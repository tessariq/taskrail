## Method

The review combined four independent questions over the spec, inherited behavior,
future-version contracts, current implementation architecture, and all existing
v0.5 task files:

1. Consistency: can two conforming implementations interpret a requirement
   differently?
2. Gaps: are actors, states, failures, recovery, compatibility, and evidence
   boundaries complete?
3. Additions: is a small adjacent behavior required to make the current outcome
   usable, rather than merely attractive future scope?
4. Adversarial: can an implementation satisfy the words while violating lifecycle,
   filesystem, Git, review, or operator intent?

Every accepted finding below was reviewed with the maintainer and incorporated
into the revised normative specs before task redecomposition.

## Structural Signal Dispositions

- `coverage` reports 20 of 20 coverable areas linked (100%) and no invalid active
  anchor. The 42 reported orphans are intentionally forward-anchored v0.6/v0.7
  tasks, not uncovered v0.5 work.
- Current `under-decomposed-area` signals are accepted mechanical bullet-count
  candidates. Each affected area is owned by one or more independently verifiable
  outcomes plus integration/release tasks where behavior crosses surfaces; splitting
  further would produce layer/file tickets rather than new outcomes.
- Current dependency-anomaly signals for T-246, T-242, T-245, T-225, and T-241 are
  accepted because each is a matrix/integration outcome whose direct consumed
  prerequisites are anchored in other areas. Adding same-area or transitive edges
  solely to cluster the graph would misstate execution requirements.
- The dependency anomaly for completed T-200 is expected historical preparation,
  not a missing edge. Dependencies record direct consumed prerequisites only;
  transitive prerequisites are not repeated as safeguards.

## Release Condition

This report authorizes planning only. It does not authorize implementation by
itself, certify reviewer independence, or satisfy the v0.5 release review. Before
release, the shipped prompt/skill/publisher flow must review the then-current spec,
publish its strict artifacts, re-run reviewed decomposition against final bytes,
and leave no unresolved current-version high or medium finding.
